Frequently asked questions

Everything people ask before installing.

How do I download an Instagram Reel?

Open the Reel on instagram.com with the extension installed. The AutoClipper button shows up in the corner of the page — click it, hit "Download video", and the MP4 is saved to your Downloads folder.

Does it download TikTok videos without watermark?

We save the original file TikTok serves to your browser and never stamp anything on it. Whatever TikTok delivers is exactly what you keep.

Why can't it download YouTube videos?

Chrome Web Store policy forbids extensions that download from YouTube. Instead, on YouTube the extension gives you a one-click shortcut to AutoClipper, which can clip, caption and export the video for you.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

Completely free, no account, no limits, no data collection. The extension is made by AutoClipper — if you ever want to turn videos into viral clips automatically, that's the product we hope you'll try.

Is downloading these videos legal?

The extension is a tool. Download only content you created, have permission to save, or that is licensed for reuse — and respect each platform's terms of service.
